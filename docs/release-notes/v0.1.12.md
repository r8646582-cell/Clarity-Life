# Clarity 0.1.12: a coach that remembers what you said, and when

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Nothing you have is changed. No new tables.

## Why
On 14 Oct you planned at 9 am to nap until 11 and do one lecture if the power was back. At 11:20 you wrote
"I woke up but the lights are still off" in a new chat, and the coach told you to go and nap. It knew the plan
but not that it was made before your nap, and a new chat could miss the chat just before it altogether. The
full story is in `docs/audits/bug-scan-2026-10-14.md`.

## The coach remembers
- **A new chat sees the one you were just in.** For 6 hours after your last message there, a new chat gets
  its last 8 messages word for word, with the time you wrote each one. Typing on Talk home after Android closed
  the app (it often does during a nap) no longer starts from nothing.
- **Times, not just days.** Earlier chats now read "Wed 14 Oct, 9:05 am to 9:40 am (today)", and things the
  search finds read "Wed 14 Oct, 9:10 am (today, 2 hours 10 minutes ago)". The app counts all of it.
- **A chat continued today counts as today's**, not the day it started.
- **A new chat catches up** as soon as the previous chat's summary is written, instead of the next day.
- **Long chats keep their beginning.** A chat's summary used to be rewritten from its newest part only; now
  each new summary starts from the old one.
- **A summary that failed tries again later** (after 6 hours, at the next app start) instead of never.
- **The coach places each message in time** before it answers (one new line in its instructions).

## Search finds more
- Three-letter words are searched now: **CAF**, nap, gym, mom.
- Forms of a word match: lecture, lectures, lecturing.
- Older memories are no longer pushed out by busy recent chats or common words.

## What I know
- Deleting or correcting a note (or a profile line or a person) now reaches a chat that is already open with
  its next message. Before, that chat kept using the old words until the next day.

## Small fixes
- "I may 2 hours study" is no longer read as the date 2 May.
- If you tap a card under an older reply, the coach is told.
- Test bench: a new scenario, "Back from the nap they planned".
