# Clarity 0.2.3: Google Gemini (Flash) background provider and separate test connections

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Database migration V8 to V9 is applied safely and automatically upon first launch.

## What is new in 0.2.3

### Google Gemini (Flash) Support
- **First-class Google Gemini preset**: Select Google Gemini under both Main Provider and Background AI Provider with automatic endpoint formatting (`https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai`).
- **One-tap model chips**: Instantly select `gemini-2.0-flash`, `gemini-1.5-flash`, or `gemini-2.5-flash`.
- **Get a key shortcut**: Directly opens Google AI Studio to grab your free API key.

### Separate Test Connection Controls
- **Test main connection**: Dedicated button in Settings under Main Provider to test your primary chat model, thinking configuration, and API key.
- **Test background connection**: Dedicated button in Settings under Background AI Provider to independently test your background model, endpoint URL, and API key.

### Performance & Cost Optimizations (from v0.2.2)
- **The "no card" signal (`[[cards: none]]`)**: Saves tokens when no action card is warranted.
- **Incremental memory updates (`NEW_NOTES`)**: Efficient note passing without freezing prefix rebuilds.
- **Single message embedding & int8 vector storage**: 4x faster vector index loading with 772 bytes/vector.
- **Talk check-in cards**: Log targets and promise resolutions directly with one tap from chat cards.
- **Tolerant quote checks**: Validates quotes while allowing minor typos and punctuation differences on word boundaries.

## What to check on your phone
1. In Settings, Coach: Under **Background AI provider**, tap **Google Gemini**, paste your free Google AI Studio key, and tap **Save**.
2. Tap the **`gemini-2.0-flash`** chip.
3. Tap **Test background connection** to verify your free background model setup.
