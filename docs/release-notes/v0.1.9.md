# Clarity 0.1.9: Hello by name, late night stays late night, fixes from the bug scan

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Nothing you have is changed. No new tables.

## Talk home
- **Hello, by name.** Talk home now says "Hello, Sam." with the name from Settings, Coach (or just "Hello."
  until one is set), the same at every hour. It used to say "Good evening." at 12:30 am.
- **A due promise shows once.** A promise that is due now is in the due card only. The "Next promise" row
  below it shows the next one still to come, instead of the same promise again.
- **No repeated words on today's step.** A target called "CAF lectures" with the unit "lectures" reads
  "2 CAF lectures", not "2 lectures · CAF lectures".

## Late night stays late night
Since 0.1.8 your day ends at midnight. Two things depended on the old 4 am end, and now work again:
- After midnight, the coach is told it is "late night", never "morning", until 4 am (or until your day
  ends, if you chose a later time).
- A promise card that says "tomorrow 9 am" at 12:40 am no longer saves straight to the next calendar day.
  It opens the time picker with the likeliest day already chosen, so you confirm which "tomorrow" you meant.

## Your day changes everywhere at once
- If you change "when your day ends" in Settings, Letters and your day, Talk home, Plan and Promises now
  switch to the new day as soon as you go back. Before, they kept the old day until the app was fully
  closed, while the coach already used the new one.

## Steadier Sunday letters and reviews
- Background writing (the weekly letter, reviews, chapters, Your story and what the coach learns after a
  talk) now waits up to 5 minutes when the AI goes quiet while it thinks. It used to give up after 2
  minutes, which could leave a letter "delayed".

## Small fixes
- In Android's notification settings, the "Weekly letter" channel now says where to turn the alert off:
  Clarity, Settings, Letters and your day, Sunday alert. It named a "Reminders" group that does not exist.
- On a goal, the week strip's heading counts every box it shows ("Last 7 days" above seven boxes, not
  "Last 3 days" for a Mon/Wed/Fri target).
- Unused code from the removed morning brief is gone; nothing you see changes.
