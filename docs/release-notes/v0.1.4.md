# Clarity 0.1.4: a calmer, wiser app, and plans that understand your ramp

Includes everything from 0.1.3 (plans are agreed in words before their card), in case you skipped it.

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.

## Your plan, understood
- **Ramps are real now.** A plan can hold steps: 2 a day for a week, then 3 for a week, then 4 until done.
  The coach writes each step's length in weeks; the app works out every date and shows them before you tap.
- **"Your plan finishes Sat 2 Jan."** The app counts, day by day, where your own steps get you, and how many
  days before your deadline. The flat "2.7 a day" is now one quiet line, not the headline.
- **No more "0%" on day one.** Your answer rate and pace only count from the day your plan starts. Before
  that, the screen says when it starts.
- Your pace keeps its history when a ramp moves to its next step.
- Plans are agreed in words first; the Save plan card comes after your yes (from 0.1.3).

## Every screen redesigned
- **Goal:** opens straight away when you have one goal. "Starts tomorrow" or "On track" at the top, then
  where your plan finishes, today's step with Check in, your steps as a timeline, the last seven days,
  progress and your next review. Pause, End, Archive and the forms are behind Edit.
- **Today:** a headline for the day ("Day 1. Start small, start today."), big number buttons, and after you
  answer, what it means: "298 left. Your plan still finishes Sat 2 Jan." It asks what got in the way only
  when you are under your minimum.
- **Talk:** today's check-in and anything due are calm cards at the top.
- **Promises:** what needs an answer first, upcoming ones as date tiles, the past folded away.
- **Reviews:** your newest letter as the main card, earlier weeks below.
- **What I know:** one note to confirm ("Is this right?"), then your sections as tiles.
- **Focus:** a new page for each focus, with its summary, its steps and Talk about this.
- **Settings:** a health check at the top (coach, reminders, backup) with the tap that fixes each problem,
  and everything else in groups that open in place.

## Your saved plan
Your current plan has only its first step (2 a day). To add your ramp, open a Plan and track chat and say:
"add my ramp: 2 a day this week, 3 next week, then 4 until the 300 are done". Say yes, and tap Save plan.
