# Clarity 0.1.0: the first signed release

The first build signed with your own release key. Stages A to E of the plan are built and tested in CI.
The phone checks listed in docs/BUILD-STATUS.md are still to do, and the two-week freeze (stage F) has not
started, which is why this is 0.1.0 and not 1.0.0.

## Read this before installing
- **This build cannot install over a test (debug) build.** Android only updates an app signed with the
  same key, and the test builds from CI were signed with a different one. If a test build is on your phone:
  1. In the old app, open Settings, Your data, set a backup passphrase and choose a folder if you have not,
     then tap **Back up now**. Write the passphrase on paper.
  2. Uninstall the old app. This erases its data, which is why step 1 comes first.
  3. Install this APK, open it, paste your API key again in Settings, Coach, then use
     **Restore from a backup** in Settings, Your data.
- From now on every release installs over this one. Never uninstall again.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.

## What is in the app
- **Talk**: chats with the coach that keep streaming when you switch apps, with Try again when a reply is
  cut off. Chat types: free talk, Untangle, Decide, Plan and track, and Focus.
- **Cards**: the coach suggests promises, plans, targets, backlogs, focuses and notes. Nothing is saved
  until you tap. Reminders only where you asked for one.
- **Plan**: goals, targets, backlogs, daily check-ins, honest pace numbers computed by the app, weekly and
  monthly reviews, and the Sunday letter.
- **What I know**: notes about you with the words they came from. Edit or delete any of them; deletions
  are final.
- **Your data**: encrypted on the phone, encrypted backups you choose, export, and Erase everything.
- Only DeepSeek, the provider you chose, is ever called. There is no backup provider.

## New in this release: a warmer, wiser coach
- **Getting to know you.** While it knows little about you, the coach gets to know you through what you
  bring (your days, the people who matter, what you hope for), never through an interview. You lead.
- **Coming back after time away** is welcomed, with no accounting for the gap.
- **Plan chats** meet the shame or worry that missed days bring up before going back to the numbers, and
  ask why a goal matters to you.
- **Decide chats** can say when a choice has no right answer, only one you can stand behind.
- **What I know** notes record your strengths as carefully as your slips, and describe what happened,
  never your character.
- **Letters and reviews** in a painful week put comfort first and figures second.
- Fixed: the prompts claimed check-ins record a time of day. They record reasons and weekdays only, so the
  coach can no longer invent patterns like "you miss evenings".
- The prompts speak of you as they/them, and the app ships with no name until you set one.
