# Clarity 1.0.0

The chat-first Clarity (the "v2" design in `docs/CLARITY-V2-BUILD-SPEC.md`). Install it over the old app; do not uninstall, or the encrypted data on the phone is lost.

## What changed for you
- **One plain chat a day.** No cards, no buttons in the chat, no chat types. The first message of a new day (it starts at 04:00) opens a new chat; the coach's memory is shared between days.
- **Three tabs: Talk, Know, Letters.** The Plan tab is gone from the screen. Settings is the gear in the chat's top bar.
- **A quiet top line** with today's date and, when there are any, how many things you are working on. Tap it to open Know.
- **Know**: the coach's notes grouped as Patterns, About you and People, plus a Working on list. "Is this right?" shows first the things the coach is not sure of. You can confirm, correct or delete for good.
- **What you are working on**: at most three active things. A date is only ever taken from a calendar date you wrote yourself; anything vaguer is asked about, never guessed. Something said in a bad moment never changes anything.
- **No signal, no problem.** A message sent with no connection waits ("Waiting to send") and is sent when the network is back.
- **"forget that"** in the chat opens a confirm sheet naming the exact note. Nothing is deleted without your tap.
- **Letters** in a mirror style (weekly, monthly, yearly) and a **milestone letter** when you finish something. Every quote is yours and every number is counted by the app.
- **Optional daily nudge** (off by default) and the optional silent Sunday letter alert.

## Spending
There is no chat limit. The app keeps token use low by caching the fixed start of every chat, sending a smaller recent-history window, turning thinking off for background memory work, and reflecting once after you have been quiet for 30 minutes. Background AI calls are capped at 12 a day (not a setting); Settings says when that is used up.

## Not in this release
- The old Plan screens, cards and planner are hidden, not deleted, and their database tables are kept unused until you approve a cleanup after a tested backup restore.
- The optional reply-checker is not built.
- Nothing here was run on a real phone by the builder. Check by hand: send a message, turn on airplane mode and send another, look at Know and Letters, create a backup and restore it on a second device.
