# Clarity 0.1.7: no morning brief, a cleaner Your story, and a coach that gets "yesterday" right

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Nothing you have is changed. No new tables.

## The morning brief is gone
- Talk home and Plan, Today no longer show "Your morning brief", and its switch is gone from Settings.
- The app no longer writes one each morning, so it makes no AI call for it.

## Your story, made cleaner
- **One title block:** "Your story", the headline, and one line saying how long it has been and since when
  (for example "Day 1 · since 13 Oct 2026"). No more pill and date in two different corners.
- **The This month / This year / All of it switch** appears only once your story is older than this month.
  Before that, all three showed exactly the same thing.
- **The short version:** once written, it reads like a quote with a thin yellow edge. Before the first one,
  a small card says when it arrives.
- **The counts** (days answered, promises kept, things overcome) are one neat card, and stay hidden until
  something has been counted, so you are not greeted by three zeros.
- **What helps you / What you're good at** are a tidy list. Under each line it says plainly
  "Seen 2 times in your own words", so the old explanation about the small number is gone. Tap a row to see
  the proof, as before.

## The coach no longer guesses "yesterday"
- Before, the coach saw plain dates ("13 Oct") and had to work out for itself how long ago that was. It once
  said you set your plan "yesterday" when you set it today.
- Now the app writes it next to every past date the coach sees: "Tue 13 Oct, 21:40 (today)",
  "12 Oct (yesterday)", "3 days ago". The coach is told to use only that, and never to guess.
