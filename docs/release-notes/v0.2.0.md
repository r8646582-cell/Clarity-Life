# Clarity 0.2.0: the max plan, phases 1 to 7

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- The APK is much bigger than before (about 150 MB more): it now carries a better meaning model (see Phase 4).
- The first open updates your database from version 5 to version 8, in three steps. Nothing you wrote is changed
  or removed: each step only adds (dates on facts, the time of a check-in, faster indexes, a target's history).
- After the first open, the app makes new meaning vectors for your whole history in the background. Leave it open
  or come back to it for a few minutes. Keyword search works the whole time.

## Why
This is everything in `docs/MAX-PLAN.md` from Phase 1 to Phase 7: the app looks after its own data, the coach is
handed more of what it needs, and it is all faster and better tested. The AI model is not part of it. Each
phase's numbers and its "not done, said plainly" list are in that file.

## Your data is safer (Phase 1)
- **Automatic backups**: weekly (or every night), keeping the newest 4 (or 8, 14, 30). Each one is read back and
  checked before an older one is removed. Settings, Your data.
- **Test your passphrase**: a Test button says whether the passphrase you type matches. A plain warning says why
  it matters: after a phone reset, your backups and that passphrase are the only way back.
- **A nightly check at 03:30** (when the battery is not low): database integrity, repairs, search catch-up,
  reminder self-check, old log clean-up, the backup when due. It never changes what you wrote and never calls the
  AI. Settings, Health shows when it last ran.
- **Reminders** are set again after a time-zone change, and checked every night: Health says "N ahead, each has
  its alarm".
- **Readable forever**: the backup format is written down in `docs/BACKUP-FORMAT.md`, and
  `tools/backup_format.py` opens a backup on any computer with your passphrase. Export now includes reviews and
  letters.

## The coach is handed more (Phases 2 and 3)
- A vague follow-up ("same again") is searched together with your last few messages.
- "In August", "last week", "since March", "3 days ago" become exact days, worked out by code; promises can be
  found by how they went (kept, broken, moved).
- Letters, reviews, chapters, goals, goal changes and check-ins with a reason are searchable now.
- Up to 12 search results instead of 5, without near-duplicates.
- New counted facts: how your check-ins went over 90 days, a reason that is growing, how your promises went, and
  (after a few weeks) the time of day you tend to answer "Not".
- Facts carry time: a profile entry shows "Since ...", and a replaced value is kept as "Before, until ...".
- A note you deleted cannot come back in other words: it is now also compared by meaning.
- Reflection looks back over your whole history, so a pattern months apart is noticed.
- On the memory exam, the evidence within the coach's reach went from 71.5% to 97.1%.

## A better meaning model (Phase 4)
- The on-phone meaning model is now gte-base-en-v1.5 (was bge-small-en-v1.5). It did best on every exam; size was
  your call ("size does not matter"). It runs on the phone; nothing leaves it.

## Faster (Phase 5)
- At ten years of data on the test emulator: a new conversation's request 5.2 s to 1.5 s, a later search 0.7 s
  to 0.4 s, the first search of a session 7.5 s to 0.6 s. Six new database indexes.
- The background vector work waits for a battery that is not low.

## Planner (Phase 6)
- Fixed: a backlog fed by two targets counted only the first one's days; days before a plan started counted as
  days left; the deadline day said "the deadline has passed"; and a few more (see the max plan).
- A target's changes are kept, so the coach can say how it has gone since a change, and whether a ramp is ahead
  or behind its steps.
- New in Plan, Goals: **Your time, next 7 days**. Set your hours per weekday and give a target the minutes one
  session takes; the card shows each day's minutes against your hours.
- Sunday reviews add the week before, the done-rate trend by week, and the time of day you answered.

## Better tested (Phase 7)
- Screen tests and screenshots of the cards, colour-contrast checks, a large-text walk on the emulator, and
  planted-bug checks on the app's own code.
- Fixed: at the largest font size the "Reminder on" chip broke into pieces; the chips now move to a second line.

## What to check on the phone
1. Open the app once after installing. The first open may take a few seconds longer.
2. The next day, Settings, Health: "Nightly check" ran and says "database ok".
3. Settings, Your data: choose a backup schedule, tap Back up now once, and tap Test next to the passphrase.
4. Plan, Goals: tap "Set your hours" on the new "Your time, next 7 days" card.
5. In Talk, ask about something from an older chat in different words: the coach should still bring it up.
