# Clarity 0.1.1: cards that show up, and a kinder coach

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** 0.1.0. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.

## Fixed: "here is your card" with no card
- The coach could say a card was there when none was. The cause: when the app sent the conversation back
  to the coach, it removed the cards from the coach's earlier replies. The coach then saw replies that
  said "tap Save plan" with no card, copied that, and stopped sending cards. Earlier replies now keep
  their cards when they are sent back.
- The card rules showed their samples in a form the app throws away. They now show the exact form.
- If the coach still talks about a card and sends none ("here is your card", "tap Save plan"), a quiet
  line under the reply says so, and the coach is told so it can send it.
- A card that was too long is fixed quietly by the coach. It no longer tells you a card "got dropped".

## A coach that helps instead of arguing
- The coach no longer argues about what the app can or cannot do, and never says a change you want
  "barely matters". When you ask for a change, it offers the card that makes it.
- Changing a saved plan: the coach offers a change card at once. If you wanted to start tomorrow, it can
  tell you to leave today unanswered, because a day with no answer is not a miss and does not lower your
  pace.
- Ramps (2 a day, then 3, then 4): the plan starts with the first step, and each next step comes as a
  change you tap at its review.
- The prompts were rewritten to rely on judgment instead of long checklists, and now cover more of life:
  grief, money worries, love, flat weeks, joy and anger, painful weeks in letters and reviews, and goals
  with no total.
