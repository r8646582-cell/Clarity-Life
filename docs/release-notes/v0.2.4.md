# Clarity 0.2.4: Gemini 3.8 Flash 1-tap chip and default background model

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Database migration V8 to V9 is applied safely and automatically upon first launch.

## What is new in 0.2.4

### Google Gemini 3.8 Flash Preset & 1-Tap Chip
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash Button**: Added `gemini-3.8-flash` as the top 1-tap button chip for both Background Model and Chat Model.
- **Default Gemini Model**: Selecting Google Gemini now automatically defaults to Google's newest `gemini-3.8-flash` model.
- **Full Model Freedom**: The model field remains completely editable free-text—you can write or paste any model name you want.

### Dedicated Test Connection Controls
- **Test main connection**: Independently tests your primary coach model and key.
- **Test background connection**: Independently tests your background model (e.g. Gemini 3.8 Flash) and key.

## What to check on your phone
1. In Settings $\rightarrow$ Coach $\rightarrow$ **Background AI provider**: tap **Google Gemini**.
2. Notice the **`gemini-3.8-flash`** button chip is selected.
3. Paste your free Google AI Studio key and tap **Save**.
4. Tap **Test background connection** to verify!
