# Clarity 1.0.2

A new mind for the coach. The app itself is the same as 1.0.1; only the prompts changed. Install it over the old app; do not uninstall, or the encrypted data on the phone is lost.

## What changed for you
- **It talks like a wise person, not a machine.** Plain words and normal contractions (I'm, it's, don't). It no longer reads the time or the app's facts back to you, and a casual "hello" at night gets a casual answer, not a check for problems.
- **The length of a reply follows your question.** Small talk gets a line or two. A real question, a decision or a plan gets a full answer.
- **Honest in both directions.** It is gentle when you are hurting, and it calls you out plainly, once, with the evidence, when you are fooling yourself, blaming others for your own choice, or repeating something you said you wanted to stop.
- **Fewer questions.** At most one per reply, and often none: it says what it thinks you meant instead of interviewing you.
- **Better plans.** It checks a plan against the hours you really have, plans for the likeliest obstacle with an "if this, then that", asks how much you want a step, and never makes up a review date.
- **After a lost day** it checks whether "I'll make it up tomorrow" really fits, finds the cause without shame, and sets one small step.
- **Memory.** The memory keeper keeps one note per subject (no old and new versions side by side) and keeps an agreed plan as one note it updates, so the coach can pick up yesterday's plan today.
- **Letters** pick what is really true about the week instead of filling every section.
- **It wants your life to be bigger than the chat.** It is glad when you spend time with people and never sets itself up as the only one who understands you.

## Check by hand
- Say "hello" late at night: the reply should be short and normal.
- Ask for a plan with a deadline and your weekly hours: it should check the hours, give a normal day and a minimum day, and ask before writing the final version.
- Tell it the same excuse twice in a week: it should name it, kindly and plainly.
- Run the test bench (Settings, Advanced) on your model and send the failures.
