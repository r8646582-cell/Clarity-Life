# Clarity 0.2.1: a home that appears at once, and no spinner when the AI service is down

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Nothing you have is changed. No new tables, no database update.

## Why
After 0.2.0 the home screen took a while to appear when the app started, and Test connection could spin for
minutes when DeepSeek was down.

## The home appears sooner, now and in 20 years
- **The home no longer waits for your whole plan.** It used to read every check-in you ever made and work out
  every pace and backlog before it showed anything, and that grew with every year. It now reads only what it
  shows: today, "Day N of your plan", and whether you have a plan. The dot on the Plan tab works the same way.
- **The meaning model loads after the home is on screen.** The 147 MB model from 0.2.0 used to load at the same
  moment as the home and compete with it for the phone. It now waits until the home has appeared (at most 10
  seconds). If you type within the first few seconds, the first reply may start a moment later.
- **Plan reads only your current goals' history.** A finished goal's check-ins are no longer read every time
  Plan opens, so Plan stays about as fast in year 20 as in year 1 for the same size of plan. Every number on
  Plan is exactly the same as before (`PlanReadsTest` builds 400 random plans both ways and compares them).

## When the AI service is down or busy
- While DeepSeek is busy it keeps the connection open and sends "keep-alive" lines for up to 10 minutes before
  it starts answering (DeepSeek's own documentation). Each one reset the app's 120-second timer, so the app kept
  waiting. Now only real pieces of the reply count. After 120 seconds with nothing real, the reply stops with
  the quiet line "The provider is busy or down and did not start the reply. Try again later." and a Try again
  button.
- **Test connection always answers within 90 seconds**, with "No answer within 90 seconds. The provider may be
  busy or down; try again later." when it got nothing.

## What to check on your phone
1. Close the app fully (swipe it away), then open it: the home should appear quickly.
2. Open Plan: the numbers should be the same as before.
3. The next time DeepSeek is down, Test connection should give up with a line within 90 seconds.
