# Clarity 0.1.11: a calmer Your story, and screens that stay tidy as they fill up

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Nothing you have is changed. No new tables.

## Your story
- **What helps you** and **What you're good at** show the three surest notes first (the ones you confirmed,
  then the ones seen most), each on at most two lines, with "Seen 9 times in your own words" under it and a
  green tick when you confirmed it. "Show 3 more" opens the rest.
- **Tapping a note no longer takes you away.** A panel slides up over Your story with the note, how sure the
  coach is, how often and when it was seen, and the newest thing you said about it. Every moment is still there,
  folded under one line ("All 9 moments"), eight at a time. That's right, Not quite (change the words) and
  Delete (asked twice) work right in the panel.
- **What you overcame** shows Then (your words) and Now. Tap it to unfold what you did in between.
- **The chart** has a few round gridlines with numbers. Tap a bar to read that week under the chart; tap it
  again to let go. Before your first check-in it says the bars start then, instead of showing an empty box.

## What I know
- The sections are one simple list with their counts on the right. An empty section shows a dash.
- A note in a section opens the same panel as in Your story.

## Plan
- **Promises:** the four answers sit two by two, so no word gets squeezed. Done has a soft yellow edge
  instead of a bright white block. The promise's words are in the plain font. Past promises show twelve at a
  time, newest first.
- **Goal:** "118 of 300 done" and the days left now sit on one clean line above the bar (they used to run
  together). A new card, **Where each pace lands**, shows what you need a day, your usual pace and your best
  fortnight, and where each one gets you, with the status line under it. Every number is counted by the app.

## Small fixes
- Talk home: the date is centred on the screen.
- Settings, Your data: the backup folder and passphrase are two-line rows, so a long folder name wraps
  instead of being cut off.
