# Clarity 0.1.8: Anthropic works, your day ends at midnight, tidier Settings

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Nothing you have is changed. No new tables.

## The Anthropic choice now works
- Before, picking Anthropic would have failed on every message: the app asked Claude to think in an old way
  that current Claude models refuse, and it kept the DeepSeek model names, which Anthropic does not know.
- Now Claude thinks the current way, and Chat thinking (Low, High, Max) sets how hard it thinks. Claude cannot
  switch thinking fully off, so Off works like Low; Settings says so when Anthropic is picked.
- Tapping Anthropic fills in Claude model names (claude-sonnet-5-5 for chat and background,
  claude-opus-5-5 for the weekly letter). Tapping DeepSeek puts the DeepSeek names back. You can still type
  any name.

## Your day ends at midnight
- New default: a new day starts at midnight, like a calendar. It used to be 4 am, so a 12:30 am check-in
  counted for the day before.
- The choices now read "Midnight, 1 am ... 6 am" instead of "12, 1 ... 6", and the line under them explains
  the one you picked.
- If you had already tapped a time, it stays as you set it: tap Midnight in Settings, Letters and your day.
- Check-ins you already saved keep the day they were saved under.

## Fewer false "spoke of a button" warnings
- The red line "The coach spoke of a button below but did not send one" appeared after ordinary words such as
  "your sleep is below seven hours" or "here it is". Now it appears only when the coach clearly talks about
  a button or card ("tap below", "tap Save plan", "here's the card").
- In a conversation, that line (and any other short note, such as "Wait for this reply to finish") now sits
  under the last reply instead of being pinned under the title, so it no longer covers words as you scroll.

## Small fixes
- "Anthropic" no longer breaks into "Anthrop / ic" on a narrow screen: the choices wrap to a new line instead.
- The thinking levels and the buttons under a note in What I know wrap the same way.
- A long model name at the top of Settings is shortened with "..." instead of squashing the line.
