# Clarity 0.1.10: a calmer Talk home, and a dot on Plan when something waits

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- Nothing you have is changed. No new tables.

## Talk home is for talking
- Talk home now shows only the date, "Hello, <your name>.", the day of your plan, the letter card when a new
  letter is unread, and the ways to start a conversation.
- Today's step and your promises are no longer on Talk home. A promise like "Watch the first CAF lecture at
  8am" and the plan step "2 CAF lectures" looked like two cards for one task there.
- You answer them where they live: **Plan, Today** for your steps and **Plan, Promises** for your promises.
  Nothing about those screens changed.

## A dot when something waits for you
- A coral dot shows on the **Plan** tab at the bottom while a step scheduled today has no answer, or a
  promise is due and not answered yet.
- The same dot shows on the **Today** or **Promises** button at the top of Plan, so you can see where it is.
- It goes as soon as you answer. A rest day asks nothing. An unread letter or review still shows the dot,
  as before.
- No notification comes with it; it is only a dot.

## Small fix
- The guide (How Clarity works) said a promise is answered with "Did it, Part or Not". Its buttons are
  **Done, Not done and Moved**, and the guide now says so.
