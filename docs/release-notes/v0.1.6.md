# Clarity 0.1.6: a calmer, clearer app, a home on Talk, and a morning brief

## How to install
- Install this APK **over** your current app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling erases your data.
- To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt attached here.
- The first time it opens, the app adds one small table for the morning brief. Nothing you have is changed.

## Every screen, redesigned
- **New fonts:** Instrument Sans for everything you read and tap, Instrument Serif for headlines, big numbers
  and the coach's longer writing (both are in the app; nothing is downloaded).
- **Calmer colours:** a darker background, cards with a fine edge, yellow only for the main button and the key
  number, a calm green for "saved" and "kept". The bottom tabs have icons.
- **No disclaimer lines.** "Counted by the app", "Written by the coach from your record" and similar lines are
  gone from the screens. The rules behind them have not changed: every number is still counted by the app,
  and every quote and number the coach writes is still checked before you see it.

## Talk has a home
- When no conversation is open, Talk shows a home instead of a blank chat: a greeting for the time of day,
  the day of your plan, your weekly letter when it is new, this morning's brief, today's step with a one-tap
  **Did it** (Part and Not open Plan, Today for the amount and the reason), the next promise, and two or
  three ways to start: pick up your last conversation, look at your plan, untangle something, or your focus.
- Typing on the home starts a new conversation. The arrow at the top of a conversation goes back home.
- In a conversation, the coach's replies sit beside a small yellow dot that gently pulses while it writes.
  Suggested cards are calmer: a coral edge instead of a solid coral block.

## New: the morning brief
- Each morning (from 6 am, or when your day starts if that is later) the coach writes a few lines about
  today: what your plan asks, anything worth noticing from yesterday, and one useful thought.
- It is shown on Talk home and at the top of Plan, Today.
- It is written only from facts the app counted, and it is checked like the weekly letter: every number must
  come from those facts and every quote must be your exact words. If it fails twice, no brief is shown that
  day. It never makes a card and never changes anything.
- No call is made on a day with nothing in it (nothing scheduled, no promise open, nothing answered
  yesterday). Turn it off in Settings, Letters and your day, Morning brief.

## Goal: a calm offer instead of a red number
- When your saved plan does not reach your goal by its date, the goal page no longer shows a red "short by"
  number. A calm card says, for example, "Your saved plan is 2 a day. Add your ramp?", with what the plan
  reaches and the pace that would finish on time.
- **Add the ramp** opens a Plan and track conversation with a message already typed for you. Nothing is sent
  until you press Send, and nothing changes until you save a plan card.

## Settings
- Grouped cards with icons, switches and real buttons (Test connection, Back up now, Restore, Open), shorter
  copy, and Erase everything as a clearly marked red-outlined button.
