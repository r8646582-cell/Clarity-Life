## 3. Unsure (could not prove; how to check)

- **Search results dropped to make room.** The volatile part of the request has a budget of 3,000 tokens, and the search results are the only part that can be cut. With several goals, many promises and a long EARLIER summary, the search results could be dropped completely. *Check:* Settings, Advanced, Health. A `dropped` count on chat requests would confirm it.
- **HyperOS delaying the summary job.** The 30-minute summary depends on WorkManager, which Xiaomi phones often hold back. If it's held back, finding 1 happens far more often than "within 30 minutes". *Check:* chat for a while, wait 40 minutes without opening the app, then open What I know and see whether that chat's title or summary has appeared.
- **The coach's own replies are never searched.** Only your messages, summaries, quotes, events, retired notes and promises go into the search index. An agreement that only the coach wrote out (you just said "ok") can't be found until a summary records it. That's by design (only your words are evidence), but it matters for remembering plans. See the ideas below.
