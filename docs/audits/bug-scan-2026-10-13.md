## 3. Unsure (could not prove; how to check)

- **The card step on Anthropic isn't in JSON mode.** `ai/OpenAiWire.kt:54` asks for JSON only from DeepSeek, and `ai/AnthropicWire.kt` ignores the JSON flag. Nothing unsafe can get through, because bad output is dropped by the strict parser (rule 3). It might mean more of the red "no button" lines on Anthropic than on DeepSeek. *Check:* use Anthropic for a few days, then compare `card_followup_none` and `card_followup_ok` in Settings, Advanced, Health.
- **The card step's 2,000-token limit on Anthropic.** On Anthropic, thinking can't be turned off. It stays at "low", and its thinking tokens count against that 2,000. A long think could cut the card off. *Check:* the same Health counts; also look for `cut_off`.
- **OkHttp's automatic retry** (`ai/AiModule.kt`, `retryOnConnectionFailure(true)`). On a dropped connection, this could in rare cases send one message to the provider twice (paid twice; still only one reply in the app). *Check:* compare token use on the provider's dashboard with Settings, Advanced, usage.
- **Two Talk home rows share the same calendar icon** ("Look at my plan" and "Help me decide something", `ui/talk/TalkHome.kt:116, 122`). I didn't check which icons the mockup uses.
