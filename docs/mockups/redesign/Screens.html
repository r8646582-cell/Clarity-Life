How Clarity works.

The coach only talks. You decide.Nothing in your goals, plans or promises changes until you tap a button on a card.
The numbers come from what you did.The app counts every day and pace from your check-ins, so a plan that can't work shows early.
It stays on your phone.Locked and encrypted. No account, no tracking, no streaks, no nagging. Only your messages to the coach leave the phone.

Clarity is locked.

Use your fingerprint or screen lock to open it.

How it holds up after a year

The same screens with a year of notes, promises and check-ins in them. Long things fold; nothing grows into a wall.

  • StoryWhat helps you and What you're good at show the three surest rows (confirmed first, then the most often seen), each on at most two lines, then "Show N more".
  • StoryTapping a note opens its proof in a sheet over the page: the note, its grade, how often and when, the newest thing you said about it, and every moment folded under one line, eight at a time. That's right, Not quite and Delete work right there.
  • StoryWhat you overcame shows Then and Now; tap to unfold what you did. The chart has round gridlines; tap a bar to read that week, tap again to let go. Past 26 weeks it counts by month.
  • KnowThe sections are one quiet list with the counts in a column; an empty one shows a dash. Notes in a section open the same proof sheet.
  • PlanPromise answers are two by two; Done has a warm edge, not a bright fill. Past promises come twelve at a time. The goal shows done and days left on one line, then where each pace lands.
  • Same rulesEvery number is the app's. The coach's cards become a Saved receipt after your tap; there is no Undo on them.
