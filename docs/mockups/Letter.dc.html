Your letter · 5 to 11 Oct

The week the evening kept winning

This week was less about the lectures than about the hour you tried to do them in. Three nights you sat down after dinner already tired, and three nights the evening won. The mornings you did use went the other way.

On Wednesday you said, "I can do it when I'm fresh, I just never am by 8." I think that was the most useful sentence of your week. It is not an excuse. It is a description of when you work, and it points at the fix.

Last week I suggested one lecture before noon on three days. You never mentioned it, so I don't know how it went, and there is no blame in that.

I might be wrong, but there is a gap worth a look. You told me sleep matters most right now, and bedtime slipped on 3 of the 5 nights you answered. The evening slump and the late nights may be one problem, not two.

The walk held on 6 of 7 days. That was the quiet success of the week, and nobody had to watch it.
