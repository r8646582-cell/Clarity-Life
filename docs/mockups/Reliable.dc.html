Keep reminders reliable.

Xiaomi phones stop apps in the background to save battery. These four settings let a reminder you set actually ring.

  • Battery saverNo restrictions
  • ! AutostartOff: reminders may not ring after a restart
    1. Tap Open settings below.
    2. Find Clarity in the list.
    3. Turn on Autostart, then come back here.
  • Alarms and remindersAllowed, so reminders ring on time
  • NotificationsAllowed
Check it for real

Lock the phone after tapping. If nothing rings in a minute, one of the settings above is still blocking it.

After the phone restarts, the app sets every reminder again on its own.
